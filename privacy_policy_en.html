3. Network connections and third parties

  • Rates provider. To update rates, the App makes an HTTPS request to open.er-api.com, operated by ExchangeRate-API (AYR Tech (Pty) Ltd, South Africa). The request does not include your name, photo, favorites or any App identifier. However, as with any Internet connection, the provider may receive and log technical data such as your IP address and the date and time of the request, under its own terms (exchangerate-api.com/terms). This involves an international transfer of that technical data.
  • Google Play. Purchase and distribution of the App are handled by Google under its privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details. Google may provide us with aggregated statistics and technical reports (for example, crashes) if your device shares diagnostic data.
  • Advertising and analytics. The App shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share data with advertisers.

4. App permissions

  • Internet and network state: download rates and detect connectivity.
  • Notifications: alert you to changes in your favorite currencies. You can deny the permission or turn them off in Settings.
  • Photos: the App does not request access to your gallery. When you choose a profile photo, the system picker is used and the App receives only the image you select.
  • Daily background task: if notifications are on and you have saved favorites, a daily task downloads rates and compares them to decide whether to alert you.

5. Screenshots and sharing

Screenshots are generated on your device. If you choose to share one, it is sent to the app you select (for example, a messaging app) and becomes subject to that app’s terms. We do not receive a copy.

6. Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed locally only to provide the features you ask for when using the App. We do not build profiles or make automated decisions about you.

7. Retention

For as long as the App is installed and until you clear its data. We keep no personal data about you on our own servers.

To exercise them or ask us anything, write to [CORREO_DE_CONTACTO]. For data processed by third parties (Google, ExchangeRate-API), please contact them directly.

9. Security

Rate downloads use HTTPS and local data resides in the App’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox. No method is 100% secure.
